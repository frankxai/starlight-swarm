# Adopt Starlight Swarm Primitives for Your Business

**Goal**: Take the generalized, ultra-advanced swarm system from this official repo and tailor it to your own companies/projects in days, not months.

## 1. Understand the Layers (from GOD-MODE-SWARM-OS-V2)

- **God Mode Model Router**: Never get stuck on one model. Declare primary + fallbacks (Claude Fable/Opus, GPT-5.6 variants, Gemini 3.6, Grok 4.5, etc.).
- **Org Structure**: Chief Queen → Domain Queens → Generals → Specialist Swarms.
- **Primitives & Topologies**: Reusable blocks (Hierarchical, Mesh, Pipeline, Ruflow, Paperclip, GStack Council, etc.).
- **Massive Action**: Bounded campaigns that drive real outcomes with measurement and gates.

## 2. Quick Start (Minimal Viable Tailor)

1. Clone or copy this repo (or just the `primitives/` and `docs/` folders you need).
2. Pick 1–3 primitives that match your highest-leverage work (e.g., Revenue Systems + Funnel + Research Pipeline).
3. Create your Domain Queen(s) — map the org chart JSON to your actual businesses.
4. Wire the Model Router config for the models you actually have access to.
5. Add your memory/governance layer (SIS equivalent or simple ledger + receipts).
6. Define your human gates (especially for money, publishing, irreversible changes).
7. Run one small bounded campaign using the pipeline or ruflow primitive.
8. Measure a real business outcome (e.g., "one verified revenue path reviewed and ready for test mode").

## 3. Recommended First Setup for a Creator / Solopreneur / Small Team

- **One Domain Queen** (you or your main brand).
- **Generals** for Revenue, Content/Product, Ops.
- **Core Swarms**:
  - Revenue/Checkout Integrity (Pipeline + strong checker)
  - Content/Funnel (Ruflow or Idea Forge)
  - Research & Synthesis (Ruflow)
  - Ops/Safety (Control Plane Sentinel + GStack reviews)
- **Router**: Start with 2–3 providers you already pay for. Always list fallbacks.

## 4. For Larger or Multi-Brand Operations

- Instantiate multiple Domain Queens (one per brand or major product line).
- Use Generals for shared functions (e.g., one Chief Revenue General across brands).
- Central Chief Queen for portfolio view and cross-domain routing.
- Heavy use of Swarm Comms Protocol between machines or team members.

## 5. Safety & Governance (Do Not Skip)

- Keep the escalation ladder: swarm → general/queen → chief → human for high-stakes actions.
- Every lane must report exact model used and whether it was a fallback.
- Use receipts, hashes, and evidence packets for everything important.
- No autonomous money movement or publishing without explicit gates.

## 6. Customization Guidelines

- **Generalized layer** (this repo): Keep portable. No private paths or credentials.
- **Your overlay**: Add your specific SKUs, domains, review checklists, brand voice, SIS mappings.
- **Fork strategy**: For heavy customization, fork the repo. For light use, copy the primitive folders you need.

## 7. Example "Massive Action" Campaign for a New User

Objective: Make one real revenue offer purchase-ready with verified checkout.

Steps:
1. Router selects Fable 5 (primary) + GPT-5.6 fallback for the Revenue Systems Swarm.
2. Run Pipeline: research current claims → audit links/code paths → server price authority check → test-mode smoke test.
3. Independent checker (Opus or equivalent) reviews.
4. Produce receipt + measurement ("3 dead links retired, one canonical Stripe CTA proven in test mode").
5. Human reviews and decides on live activation.
